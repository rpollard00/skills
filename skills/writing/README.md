# Writing

Skills for writing and editing text, and for routing writing tasks to the right style discipline.

## Model-invoked

- **[writing-router](./writing-router/SKILL.md)** — Must-use router for any writing or editing task. Sends technical text to simple-technical-english and general prose to unslop.
- **[simple-technical-english](./simple-technical-english/SKILL.md)** — ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English for docs, runbooks, error messages, and release notes.
- **[unslop](./unslop/SKILL.md)** — Removes AI tells and adds human voice for general prose, blog voice, and marketing copy.
